About & Methodology

Data science methodologies, scoring mathematics, privacy principles, and transparent benchmark results.

How the SkillGap score is calculated

SkillGap computes an explainable, deterministic score from 0 to 100 representing how well a candidate's resume covers the skill demands of a specific job description. Rather than using an uninterpretable deep neural network, our scoring model breaks down into four explicit components:

60% · Exact Match

The proportion of job description skills found directly in the candidate's resume using word-boundary alias matching.

20% · Partial Match

Credit granted for related skills (e.g., PyTorch for Deep Learning, PostgreSQL for SQL) via cosine similarity and ontology mapping.

10% · Category Coverage

Measures breadth across foundational domains (Programming, Databases, Cloud, Machine Learning, Visualization, Statistics).

10% · Resume Quality

Heuristic score based on structured section headers (Skills, Experience, Projects, Education) and extraction confidence.

Grade scale: A (90+) B (75–89) C (55–74) D (35–54) F (<35)

Data science techniques used

  • PDF Parsing & Section Recognition: PyMuPDF extracts raw text without OCR latency. Regular expressions detect key resume blocks (Skills, Experience, Projects, Education) for section-weighted confidence scoring.
  • Rule-Based Boundary Extraction: Look-around regular expressions with word boundary guards prevent substring false positives (e.g. distinguishing 'Excel' from 'excellent', 'Java' from 'JavaScript', 'Git' from 'digital', and 'ML' from 'HTML'). Special terms (C++, C#, Node.js, CI/CD, scikit-learn) have dedicated token rules.
  • TF-IDF Skill Importance Weighting: Scikit-learn TF-IDF vectorizers fitted on an industry job corpus weigh rare specialized skills higher than ubiquitous baseline terminology.
  • Sub-word & Character n-gram Cosine Similarity: Partial matches calculate character 2-4 gram TF-IDF cosine similarity to recognize near-synonyms, coupled with domain relation mappings.
  • Topological Prerequisite Sorting: NetworkX directed acyclic graphs (DAGs) model skill prerequisites to generate logically ordered learning sequences.
  • Clustering & Dimensionality Reduction: Scikit-learn PCA projects high-dimensional skill spaces to 2D coordinates alongside K-Means (k=5) cluster segmentation.

Important limitations

The SkillGap score is NOT a hiring prediction:

  • A high score does not guarantee interviews or job offers. Hiring decisions depend on leadership, communication, problem-solving, culture fit, and practical project depth.
  • The system evaluates textual alignment between the resume and the job description. It does not verify verified competence or portfolio credibility.
  • PDFs that are purely flattened images (scanned documents) without embedded text layers cannot be read and will yield 0 extracted skills.

Privacy statement

SkillGap is an educational course project designed with strict privacy boundaries:

  • Your resumes and job descriptions are processed locally or on your deployed instance.
  • We do not sell, rent, or distribute candidate resumes or personal data to third-party recruiters or commercial platforms.
  • You can permanently delete your uploaded resumes, generated analyses, and entire account at any time from the Account Settings page.
